Email list (only if you sign up)

If you tick the consent box and submit your email address, we use it to send you emails about this book. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email, or by writing to us. We never add you to a list without your consent.

Analytics

This site sets no cookies. If aggregate statistics are switched on, we use a cookieless, privacy-friendly counter (Plausible) that records page views and clicks on the sample, signup and checkout buttons, with no personal data and no cross-site tracking.

What we do not do

We use no advertising cookies. We do not sell your data or send you marketing email unless you ask for it.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when you open a page. Card payments are handled by our payment provider under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card number. Your bank processes bank transfers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Why and how long

We use your data to fulfil your order and answer you (contract and legitimate interest). We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and other emails for up to 24 months.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to its use, by emailing us. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Ireland this is the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).
