The author

Alexandru Ureche

Ureche Ionel Alexandru, known as Alex, comes from Măgura Ilvei, in Bistrița-Năsăud County, Romania. He studied Orthodox Pastoral Theology at Babeș-Bolyai University in Cluj-Napoca (2021–2025), and Dublin is part of his path.

His interests run through mindset, philosophy, faith and business, and he writes about how they meet in a life that is lived and not only imagined. The Lantern Hour is a business fable built around those questions.

Why this book

"I did not write this book from the top of a mountain. I wrote it from the middle of the climb." The questions in it are the ones the author kept meeting on his own road: what is ambition for, if faith is real? How does a person stay disciplined without becoming hard? What do we owe to money, and what does it owe to us?
